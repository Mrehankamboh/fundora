01 What is the difference between 1-Step and 2-Step?

1-Step is a single evaluation phase with a higher profit target. 2-Step splits the journey into two phases with a lower initial target and an additional verification stage.

02 Are there overnight or news trading restrictions?

Overnight positions are permitted. Extreme news windows may carry elevated risk — trade within the published daily loss limit at all times.

03 When do I receive my first reward?

After your first successful payout cycle on a funded account, the evaluation fee is refunded with your reward per program terms.

04 Can I trade EAs and indicators?

Yes on MetaTrader 5, provided they do not exploit latency, arbitrage, or reverse-copy prohibited strategies outlined in the terms.

05 How does account scaling work?

Consistent profitable cycles unlock larger allocations, up to the published maximum for your program tier.

06 Which platforms can I use with Fundora?

Trade on MetaTrader 5, cTrader, or manage progress in the Fundora Portal. Objectives stay the same across every platform.
