Step 1 · Front side

Guide for the front of your document.

  1. 1Lay the front side flat on a dark surface.
  2. 2Keep all four corners inside the frame.
  3. 3Avoid glare, shadows, and blur.
  4. 4Do not cover the photo, name, or document number.

The camera opens for the front side.

Step 2 · Back side

Guide for the back of your document.

  1. 1Turn the document over. Do not crop the edges.
  2. 2Keep text, numbers, and barcodes readable.
  3. 3Use the same light as the front photo.
  4. 4Hold the camera still until the picture is sharp.

The camera opens for the back side.

Step 3 · Selfie

Guide for a live photo of your face. An administrator reviews it.

  1. 1Face the camera straight on.
  2. 2Remove hats, masks, and sunglasses.
  3. 3Put the light on your face, not behind you.
  4. 4Use a plain background and a neutral expression.
  5. 5Fit your face inside the oval, then capture.

The front camera opens for the selfie.
